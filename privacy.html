Ad measurement on your device

We use Google Ads on-device conversion measurement, part of the Google Analytics for Firebase SDK inside the app, to find out whether one of our ads led you to install Shopwise. It works in two ways:

  • For everyone, signed in or not. Google states that the SDK uses de-identified, temporary app event data on your device, derived from signals such as IP address and timestamps. This is built into Google's SDK, and the app has no setting to switch it off.
  • When you sign in or create an account. The app turns your account email into a one-way hash (SHA-256) and gives it to the SDK, which matches it on your device. The sign-in screen tells you this. To avoid it, use Shopwise without an account; signing out stops any further matching. Where Google's consent message is shown (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app only does this if you allow it in that message, and you can change your choice under Account Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

Google states that your email is never sent to Google in readable form, and that nothing leaves your device in a way that identifies you or your device: Google receives only a de-identified result, such as "an ad led to an install", and handles it under its own privacy policy. Google reports at most one match per user. Signing out, deleting your account or changing your choice later does not withdraw a match that has already been reported.
