Support

Questions, answered

Is Shopwise free?

Yes. Shopwise is free to download and use. An optional in-app upgrade removes ads and lifts the limits on saved deals, saved leaflets and loyalty cards.

Which stores are included?

Shopwise shows the current leaflets and specials from South Africa's major grocery, pharmacy and home retailers, refreshed as new leaflets are published.

Are the prices accurate?

Prices are those advertised by the retailer and can change. Always check at the till.

Is Shopwise linked to the retailers?

No. Shopwise is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any retailer whose leaflets it shows.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account Settings → Delete Account. That removes your account, your lists, your saved leaflets and your loyalty cards. See the Privacy Policy for details.
